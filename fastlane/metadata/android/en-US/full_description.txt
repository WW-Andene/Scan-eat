Scan'eat helps you understand what you eat — in the context of who you are.

SCAN AND UNDERSTAND
Scan a product's barcode or photograph its label to get a clear nutrition score, with the exact reasons behind every point gained or lost.

TAILORED TO YOUR PROFILE
Declare your allergens, diet, and health conditions (diabetes, pregnancy, kidney disease, IBS, Crohn's disease, and more): every score, recommendation, and warning adjusts accordingly — no generic advice.

TRACK YOUR JOURNAL
Meals, weight, hydration, activity, intermittent fasting, medication, and food spending — all in one journal, with "recommended / not recommended" suggestions tailored to your profile.

SEARCH FOR ANYTHING
Search covers your own scan history, a curated reference database, and — to go further — an online search by name, ingredient, or additive against Open Food Facts, with links to official sources (USDA, EFSA, ANSES/CIQUAL, PubChem).

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No account, no central server. Your profile and health data are encrypted and stored on your device only.

Scan'eat does not provide medical advice — it's an information tool, not a substitute for a healthcare professional.
